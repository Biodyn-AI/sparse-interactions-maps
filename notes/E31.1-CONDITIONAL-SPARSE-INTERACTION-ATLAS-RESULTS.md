# E31.1 conditional sparse-interaction atlas — results

Date: 2026-08-21

## Bottom line

The preregistered claim failed. A single sparse, saturation-corrected pair graph learned on 16 discovery prompts did not transfer to 32 fresh prompts in the primary GPT-2-small IOI regime (10 non-hub sublayers already patched, layer-0 MLP off).

This does **not** mean that the interaction energy is dense everywhere. On the confirmation prompts themselves, the best 24 of 253 pairs contained 69.1% of the corrected interaction energy. The failure is portability: the 24 discovery-selected pairs contained only 12.7% of fresh-prompt energy and overlapped only 3 of the fresh top 24.

## Registered primary result

- Top-24 fresh energy coverage: **0.1272** (required 0.60).
- Top-24 fraction of oracle coverage: **0.1841** (required 0.75).
- Top-48 fresh energy coverage: **0.2520** (required 0.80).
- Discovery/confirmation interaction-energy correlation: **0.1303**.
- Discovery/confirmation signed-interaction correlation: **0.2283** (required 0.50).
- Discovery/fresh top-24 overlap: **3/24**; sign agreement: **17/24**.
- The selected support was only at the 56.8th percentile of 10,000 jointly matched random supports.
- Only 2 of 9 registered checks passed, so the primary, hierarchical, and overall atlas verdicts are **fail**.

An independent replay reproduced the complete score exactly. No scorer, hash-chain, query-accounting, or custody defect was found.

## Positive empirical structure

The uncorrected finite-intervention interactions transferred much better in the same primary regime:

- Discovery top-24 fresh coverage: **0.6142**.
- Top-24 fraction of fresh oracle coverage: **0.9078**.
- Discovery/fresh top-24 overlap: **18/24**.
- Energy Pearson correlation: **0.6373**; rank Spearman correlation: **0.7546**.
- The leading edges were predominantly attention–attention redundancies, especially L5-attention × L8-attention, followed by interactions among L5 and L8–L11 attention blocks.

These raw second differences are real causal nonadditivities, but they can combine mechanism-specific interaction with generic endpoint saturation. They therefore support a conditional redundancy graph, not yet a direct communication graph.

The clearest descriptive conditional regime was the high-occupancy, hub-on state (18 other non-hub sublayers already patched):

- Top-24 coverage: **0.7787**; top-48: **0.8402**.
- Discovery/fresh energy correlation: **0.9287**.
- Effective support: **7.31**; 66/253 edges were needed for 90% coverage under the discovery order.
- The leading structure was a long-range attention backbone centered on L0-attention with L9–L11 attention.
- For layer-0-MLP spokes in the same high-occupancy regime, 7/23 discovery-ranked spokes recovered 90% of fresh energy, led by L0-MLP with L10/L11 attention.

This regime was descriptive rather than confirmatory, so it motivates a fresh test rather than a claim.

## Why the primary correction failed

The registered arcsinh saturation null was not portable across prompt panels. In the primary regime, discovery selected curvature 8, while a post-hoc fit on confirmation selected curvature 1. Using the over-strong frozen correction amplified a different set of fresh edges and destroyed support transfer. Raw interactions and the high-occupancy regime, where the selected curvature was exactly zero, were substantially more stable across templates, directions, and background covers.

The correct conclusion is therefore:

> A universal portable sparse corrected graph failed, but conditional sparse interaction structure remains plausible and becomes much stronger in specific causal states. The graph must include regime or state, rather than assigning one fixed edge weight to each pair.

## Next experiment

Do not scale the model or add a low-rank tail yet. First test whether the repeatable coarse attention edges resolve into a genuinely sparse head graph. E31.2 will exhaustively measure all 12×12 head pairs inside three discovery-frozen block pairs—L5-attention × L8-attention, L8-attention × L9-attention, and L5-attention × L10-attention—on fresh k10/hub-off backgrounds. Discovery will select one 4×4 head rectangle per block pair before a separate fresh confirmation panel is opened. The primary tests are held-out energy coverage, matched-random enrichment, rank/sign stability, and whether the selected heads reconstruct the measured whole-block interaction better than singleton-selected heads.
