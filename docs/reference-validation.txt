REFERENCE-BY-REFERENCE ONLINE VALIDATION
Checked 17 November 2026

Each reference was explicitly checked online for bibliographic identity, available publication metadata, and relevance to its cited statement. Primary publisher/proceedings/preprint pages or publisher-registered DOI metadata were used. Access limitations are recorded per entry. This is not an independent replication of cited studies or an exhaustive literature review.

[1] Towards Automated Circuit Discovery for Mechanistic Interpretability (2023)
Key: conmy2023acdc
Primary source: https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/34e1dbe95d34d7ebaf99b9bcaeb5b2be-Abstract-Conference.html
Checked: 2026-11-17; status: validated_primary_sources
Cited relevance: Automates finding connections between components using activation-patching-based circuit discovery; suitable for the existing circuit-discovery comparison, not evidence that our pair scores recover direct edges.

[2] Have Faith in Faithfulness: Going Beyond Circuit Overlap When Finding Model Mechanisms (2024)
Key: hanna2024faithfulness
Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.17806
Checked: 2026-11-17; status: validated_primary_sources
Cited relevance: Introduces EAP-IG and shows why overlap with known components does not substitute for testing retained-circuit faithfulness. Directly supports separating localization from behavior preservation.

[3] AtP*: An efficient and scalable method for localizing LLM behaviour to components (2024)
Key: kramar2024atp
Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.00745
Checked: 2026-11-17; status: validated_primary_sources
Cited relevance: Studies scalable gradient approximations to activation patching, their false negatives and diagnostic corrections. Supports efficiency-versus-validation discussion, not a claim to infer direct wiring.

[4] The Curse of Multiple Mediators: Hidden Interaction Effects in Activation Patching (2026)
Key: vaidyanathan2026mediators
Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27510
Checked: 2026-11-17; status: validated_primary_sources
Cited relevance: Derives interaction terms in activation-patching effects, examines GPT-2 IOI and higher-order interactions, and explains state-dependent component effects. Acknowledge as prior interaction theory; do not present our finite-difference interaction as a newly discovered causal principle.

[5] Conditional Co-Ablation: Recovering Self-Repair Backups in Transformer Circuits (2026)
Key: gong2026coax
Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01940
Checked: 2026-11-17; status: validated_primary_sources
Cited relevance: Finds backup components that become important after primary components are removed, with GPT-2 IOI and broader experiments. Supports the concrete compensation example; it is not the same score or claim as direct-edge discovery.

[6] Attention Is All You Need (2017)
Key: vaswani2017attention
Primary source: https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2017/hash/3f5ee243547dee91fbd053c1c4a845aa-Abstract.html
Checked: 2026-11-17; status: validated_primary_online
Cited relevance: Original Transformer architecture; supports the architectural provenance of attention and feed-forward components, not any claim that our intervention score identifies direct wiring.

[7] Language Models Are Unsupervised Multitask Learners (2019)
Key: radford2019language
Primary source: https://cdn.openai.com/better-language-models/language_models_are_unsupervised_multitask_learners.pdf
Checked: 2026-11-17; status: validated_primary_online
Cited relevance: Primary description of GPT-2 and its next-token language-model training; supports model provenance, not our measured interaction results.

[8] Attribution Patching Outperforms Automated Circuit Discovery (2024)
Key: syed2024attributionpatching
Primary source: https://aclanthology.org/2024.blackboxnlp-1.25/
Checked: 2026-11-17; status: validated_primary_proceedings
Cited relevance: Uses a linear approximation to activation patching to score and prune edges, an explicit edge-localization/discovery baseline precedent. Do not equate its derivative-based edge scores with the present finite pair-interaction score.

[9] Interpretability in the Wild: a Circuit for Indirect Object Identification in GPT-2 small (2023)
Key: wang2023ioi
Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.00593
Checked: 2026-11-17; status: validated_primary_sources
Cited relevance: Provides the IOI task and a component-level circuit evaluated for faithfulness, completeness and minimality. Supports the existing task provenance and the need to check circuit behavior beyond a shortlist.

[10] In-context Learning and Induction Heads (2022)
Key: olsson2022induction
Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.11895
Checked: 2026-11-17; status: validated_primary_sources
Cited relevance: Concrete mechanistic case study of induction heads performing repeated-token continuation. Cite as an example of explaining a computation, with strong causal evidence for small attention-only models and correlational evidence for larger models with MLPs; not a universal proof about in-context learning.

[11] Progress measures for grokking via mechanistic interpretability (2023)
Key: nanda2023grokking
Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.05217
Checked: 2026-11-17; status: validated_primary_sources
Cited relevance: Reverse engineers modular-addition computation using Fourier structure and confirms it with activation/weight analysis and Fourier-space ablations. Good example of a stronger algorithm-level explanation than score repeatability alone.

[12] How does GPT-2 compute greater-than?: Interpreting mathematical abilities in a pre-trained language model (2023)
Key: hanna2023greaterthan
Primary source: https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/efbba7719cc5172d175240f24be11280-Abstract-Conference.html
Checked: 2026-11-17; status: validated_primary_sources
Cited relevance: Explains a GPT-2 circuit for choosing valid two-digit end years, including the role of late MLPs, and checks related tasks. Another concrete example that a circuit explanation describes what components compute, not just which pairs score highly.

[13] Locating and Editing Factual Associations in GPT (2022)
Key: meng2022rome
Primary source: https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2022/hash/6f1d43d5a82a37e89b0665b33bf3a182-Abstract-Conference.html
Checked: 2026-11-17; status: validated_primary_sources
Cited relevance: Uses causal interventions to localize factual-recall computations and tests a proposed role by editing feed-forward weights. Supports the distinction between localizing an effect and testing a concrete mechanism; it does not validate our pair-score method.

[14] Causality: Models, Reasoning, and Inference (2009)
Key: pearl2009causality
Primary source: https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/causality/B0046844FAE10CBF274D4ACBDAEB5F5B
Checked: 2026-11-17; status: validated_primary_publisher
Cited relevance: Provides the causal-model distinction between association and intervention and the structural vocabulary for direct versus mediated effects. Use only as foundational framing; it does not prove anything specific about neural pair-score maps.

[15] Causal Abstractions of Neural Networks (2021)
Key: geiger2021causalabstractions
Primary source: https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2021/hash/4f5c422f4d49a5a807eda27434231040-Abstract.html
Checked: 2026-11-17; status: validated_primary_proceedings
Cited relevance: Tests alignment between interpretable causal variables and neural representations through interchange interventions. Supports explaining that an internal causal hypothesis requires testing intervention behavior, not merely naming highly ranked components.

[16] Causal Abstraction: A Theoretical Foundation for Mechanistic Interpretability (2025)
Key: geiger2025causalabstraction
Primary source: https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v26/23-0058.html
Checked: 2026-11-17; status: validated_primary_journal
Cited relevance: Formalizes causal abstraction and graded faithfulness, and connects activation/path patching, circuit analysis and other methods within one framework. Grounds the distinction between an interventional effect and a faithful higher-level explanation.

[17] Finding Alignments Between Interpretable Causal Variables and Distributed Neural Representations (2024)
Key: geiger2024distributedalignment
Primary source: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v236/geiger24a.html
Checked: 2026-11-17; status: validated_primary_proceedings
Cited relevance: Introduces distributed alignment search: learned alignments need not correspond to disjoint sets of neurons. Relevant to the choice of computational units and why using native heads/MLPs is a limitation, not a universal decomposition.

[18] Investigating Gender Bias in Language Models Using Causal Mediation Analysis (2020)
Key: vig2020causalmediation
Primary source: https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2020/hash/92650b2e92217715fe312e6fa7b90d82-Abstract.html
Checked: 2026-11-17; status: validated_primary_proceedings
Cited relevance: Uses causal mediation and controlled changes to neurons/attention heads to distinguish information encoded internally from information used in model behavior. Supports the historical and conceptual introduction to internal interventions.

[19] Towards Best Practices of Activation Patching in Language Models: Metrics and Methods (2024)

Checked: 2026-11-17; status: validated_primary_conference_pdf
Cited relevance: Shows that corruption methods and evaluation metrics can yield different localization conclusions. Direct support for fixing and disclosing response metrics, replacement schemes and backgrounds rather than treating patch scores as invariant model properties.

[22] How Important Is a Neuron? (2019)

[25] Axiomatic Attribution for Deep Networks (2017)

[26] A Unified Approach to Interpreting Model Predictions (2017)

Checked: 2026-11-17; status: verified_online_primary
Cited relevance: SHAP is an established framework for additive per-feature explanations of predictions. It helps define the distinction between an output explanation and an internal circuit description.

[27] Detecting Statistical Interactions from Neural Network Weights (2018)

[28] Explaining Explanations: Axiomatic Feature Interactions for Deep Networks (2021)

Cited relevance: Integrated Hessians is a principled approach to pairwise input-feature interactions. It establishes that interaction explanations are existing research, while leaving our internal-part and selected-circuit checks distinct.

[29] How does This Interaction Affect Me? Interpretable Attribution for Feature Interactions (2020)

Checked: 2026-11-17; status: verified_online_primary
Cited relevance: Archipelago explicitly separates detecting nonadditive interactions from attributing a prediction to interacting groups. This reinforces that interaction strength and an explanatory claim need not be interchangeable.

The paper reports no substantial language-model interpretability/sparsity improvement, so do not claim LIB solved large-language-model decomposition.
